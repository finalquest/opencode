Ask a stronger advisor model for a second opinion when you are uncertain or blocked.

Use seek_advice when:
- your confidence is low
- there are multiple valid approaches and you must choose
- an architecture decision is involved
- debugging is inconclusive
- you are reviewing risks before major edits

Do NOT use seek_advice for:
- trivial edits
- reading files (use read)
- running tests (use bash)
- simple refactors

The advisor can read and search the codebase but cannot edit, write, or run commands. It returns a structured recommendation (summary, recommendation, reasoning, risks, next_steps, confidence) — it does not do the work for you. You remain responsible for acting on the advice.
